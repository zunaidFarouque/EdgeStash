# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The EdgeStash community is committed to providing a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and users, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected Standards

We ask everyone who participates in the project—through issues, pull requests, discussions, or code reviews—to uphold the following standards:

- **Respect and Kindness**: Treat everyone with courtesy and empathy. Disagreements should focus on technical merits and project design, not individuals.
- **Constructive Collaboration**: Offer actionable feedback, be receptive to suggestions, and acknowledge the contributions of others.
- **Focus on Project Quality**: Support our shared goals of rock-solid stability, zero idle overhead, and high-performance engineering.
- **Welcoming Newcomers**: Assist new contributors in understanding the codebase, test harness, and architectural invariants.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in any project space:
- Harassing, derogatory, or discriminatory language or behavior.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing private personal information of others without permission.
- Disruptive or bad-faith behavior that derails productive development.

## Enforcement and Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. If you observe unacceptable behavior, please reach out directly to the maintainer at `zunaidfarouque@gmail.com`. All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.
